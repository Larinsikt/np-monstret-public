# NP-Monstret — Public Documentation

Official documentation for [NP-Monstret](https://npmonstret.se), a Swedish learning platform for preparing students for the national tests (*nationella prov*), built by certified teachers and special education specialists.

This repository is our open-source source of truth. Here we publish how the platform works, how it aligns with the Swedish National Agency for Education (Skolverket), and how we handle student data.

## About the Swedish national tests

The Swedish national tests (*nationella prov*) are mandatory standardised tests administered in years 3, 6 and 9 of compulsory school, and in upper secondary school courses. They are designed and managed by Skolverket (the Swedish National Agency for Education) and assess core skills in mathematics, Swedish, English and science.

NP-Monstret is built specifically to prepare students for these tests through targeted, individualised practice.
